## Senior Game UI & Multimedia Designer
Full Time·10 days ago
[Sign in to apply](https://hitmarker.net/login)
### Realistic Shooter / Unreal Engine
We are looking for a talented and hands-on **Game UI & Multimedia Designer** to join our team working on a mobile-first realistic shooter built in Unreal Engine 5.
The main focus of this role is creating **high-quality, visually impressive UI for a modern realistic shooter**. You will design everything from HUDs and gameplay interfaces to menus, inventories, loadouts, matchmaking, mission screens and progression systems.
You will work closely with our game designers and Unreal developers, who will bring your designs into the game. You should therefore have a good understanding of **Unreal Engine 5 and UMG** , but this is primarily a **visual design role** — we are looking for someone whose strongest skill is creating exceptional game UI, rather than programming or technical implementation.
Alongside game UI, you will also create **gameplay clips, trailers, motion graphics and promotional content** using footage captured from the game.
### **What we are looking for**
  * Strong professional experience in **game UI design** , ideally for shooters, action games or realistic games
  * A strong portfolio showing **polished, modern HUDs and in-game UI**
  * Excellent understanding of the visual language used in **realistic and military-style games**
  * Ability to design complete game interfaces, including HUDs, menus, inventories, loadouts, mission screens, progression systems and other gameplay UI
  * Strong UI/UX fundamentals, combining visual quality with readability and gameplay usability
  * Good understanding of **Unreal Engine 5 and UMG** , with the ability to prepare designs and assets for efficient implementation by our Unreal team
  * Strong knowledge of **Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma, After Effects, Premiere** or equivalent tools
  * Ability to create **animated UI, motion graphics and UI transitions**
  * Ability to capture and edit compelling **gameplay footage, gameplay clips and trailers**
  * Good understanding of cinematic editing, pacing, sound and presentation for game marketing content
  * Comfortable using **AI tools** for ideation, design exploration and content creation
  * Strong visual eye and exceptional attention to detail
  * Highly organized and systematic when managing source files, assets, versions and deliverables


### **Most important**
We are looking for someone who can take a gameplay requirement, concept or rough wireframe and transform it into a **visually outstanding, production-ready game UI**.
Your portfolio should show UI that could belong in a **high-quality modern shooter**. We are not primarily looking for candidates whose experience is focused on websites, mobile apps or corporate interfaces.
**Location:** B2B offshore/remote.
If this sounds like you, we would love to see your **portfolio and CV** , together with your salary expectations. Please send them to **hr@ibloxx.com**.
