**Who We Are  
**
  

Applied Materials is the global leader in materials science and engineering solutions that are at the foundation of virtually every new semiconductor chip and advanced display in the world. The equipment that we create and service is essential to advancing AI and accelerating the commercialization of next-generation semiconductor chips. Join us and push the boundaries of materials science and engineering in a company at the foundation of the electronics industry. The work we do together advances the world’s technology.
  
  

**What We Offer

Salary:

Location:
Dresden,DEU 
You’ll benefit from a supportive work culture that encourages you to learn, develop, and grow your career as you take on challenges and drive innovative solutions for our customers. We empower our team to push the boundaries of what is possible—while learning every day in a supportive leading global company. Visit our Careers website to learn more.
At Applied Materials, we care about the health and wellbeing of our employees. We’re committed to providing programs and support that encourage personal and professional growth and care for you at work, at home, or wherever you may go. Learn more about our benefits .
  
As a Field Service Engineer [Customer Engineer] at Applied Materials, you serve as the direct liaison with customers, collaborating closely to install, maintain, and upgrade equipment. You'll use digital analytics for troubleshooting and apply basic diagnostic techniques to assess and address technical issues. Your responsibilities include performing preventative and corrective maintenance on various systems such as electrical, vacuum, mechanical, plasma, hydraulic, and gas systems. Additionally, you'll coordinate and communicate directly with customers to ensure smooth operations and exceptional service.   

As an **Installation Engineer** , you are part of the **Field Service Operations (FSO), Customer Engineer Team** , and you will play a key role in bringing cutting-edge semiconductor equipment online at some of the world's most advanced manufacturing facilities. Working alongside experienced Customer Engineers, you'll gain hands-on exposure to complex technologies, solve challenging technical problems, and directly impact customer success.
This is an exciting opportunity for engineers who enjoy hands-on technical work, customer interaction, international travel, and working in diverse manufacturing environments.
**Travel:** Up to 75% to customer sites across the region
  

As part of our comprehensive development program, you will complete an **initial six-month onboarding and training phase**. Following this, there may be an **opportunity for a unique 12-month international training assignment in Taiwan**. This immersive experience will provide extensive technical learning, practical field exposure, and the opportunity to collaborate with global experts.
**What You'll Do**
  * Partner with Senior Customer Engineers to install, commission, maintain, and upgrade Applied Materials equipment at customer sites.
  * Support equipment startup and acceptance activities to ensure successful deployment and operational readiness.
  * Utilize digital diagnostic tools, equipment analytics, and troubleshooting methodologies to identify and resolve technical issues.
  * Perform preventive and corrective maintenance on advanced electrical, vacuum, mechanical, plasma, hydraulic, and gas delivery systems.
  * Diagnose equipment performance issues and implement solutions with support from senior team members and technical experts.
  * Build strong relationships with customers through professional communication and technical expertise .
  * Maintain compliance with all safety, quality, and operational procedures while working in customer manufacturing environments.
  * Document maintenance activities, service reports, and technical findings accurately and professionally.
  * Support continuous improvement initiatives and other business needs as required .
