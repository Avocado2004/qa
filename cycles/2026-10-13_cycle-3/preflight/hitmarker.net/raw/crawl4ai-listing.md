## Realistic 3D Characters Art Director
Full Time / Contract·1 day ago
[Sign in to apply](https://hitmarker.net/login)
> **Next great challenge is coming:**
> We are looking for a Realistic 3d Characters Art Director who will be responsible for leading the artistic and strategic development of the team by optimizing workflows, exploring new service opportunities, and ensuring the effective execution of team and project goals.
## Superpowers you have are:
  * 3+ years of experience as a Realistic 3d Characters team lead, project lead or similar position;
  * Strong understanding of artistic and technical aspects of characters creation;
  * Deep industry knowledge;
  * Strong personal artistic and technical skills;
  * High quality standards and artistic taste;
  * Feedback mastery;
  * Strategic thinking, desire to develop the team;
  * Constant self-development and the desire to develop others;
  * Ability to work under stressful conditions and solve difficult problems;
  * Good management skills;
  * Excellent communication and teamwork skills.


## As part of Room 8 studio, you will:
  * Define and drive long-term Realistic 3d Characters team development strategy;
  * Set standards for workflows and processes in collaboration with Team Leads;
  * Work with Sub-Division Director and Team Leads to align goals with overall strategy;
  * Collaborate with Division Art Director and Art Executive Development Director on Sub-Division structure and organization;
  * Initiate and implement changes to policies, workflows, and documentation;
  * Oversee and support strategically important projects;
  * Plan team structure and size based on production needs and recruitment goals;
  * Participate in the hiring process: create and assess test tasks, conduct technical interviews, evaluate candidate skills;
  * Lead recruitment, onboarding, and performance assessment of Team Leads;
  * Develop and implement onboarding and learning programs for Team Leads and the Sub-Division;
  * Conduct syncs, performance reviews, and mentorship initiatives;
  * Provide feedback to Producers, Project Managers, Team Leads, and other leadership roles;
  * Set development goals and define educational/training needs with Team Leads;
  * Organize workshops and training sessions;
  * Participate in project planning, estimation, and team assignment;
  * Resolve technical or artistic challenges and internal team conflicts;
  * Join client calls for project initiation, clarification, or issue resolution;
  * Participate in retrospectives and continuous improvement discussions;
  * Define or develop pipelines for complex and strategic projects;
  * Work on complex artistic tasks and guide optimization of processes;
  * Develop project documentation and guides;
  * Oversee the delivery of key project assets to ensure quality;
  * Integrate new tools to enhance project efficiency and team productivity;
  * Represent the Sub-Division in key client meetings and strategic discussions.

Get even more!
  * Competitive financial reward (yes, we are fair enough);
  * Challenges to raise your XP score (professional training and conferences, internal mentorships, English courses);
  * Private medical care, paid vacation, and sick leave;
  * A culture of diversity and inclusion that unites the most outstanding talents;
  * A community of people who understand and share your passion for games.
