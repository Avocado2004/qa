#### Vollständige Stellenbeschreibung
  
  
Exyte is seeking an experienced QA/QC Manager (m/f/d) for a large-scale project in Germany. The role is responsible for leading all project quality activities across CSA and MEP disciplines, as well as managing the site quality team. Close collaboration with internal stakeholders and external partners is essential to ensure the highest quality standards throughout project execution.   
  
Explore your tasks and responsibilities   
  

  * Identify and analyze customer-specific requirements, and recommend process improvement strategies across all projects.
  * Develop and standardize QA program for projects according internal and customer requirements.
  * Identifying process improvements that will increase quality with projects, and provide process improvement ideas to the Regional Quality Management Department.Lead, manage, and coordinate all quality-related functions within the project.
  * Gather and organize statistical quality data across all projects.

Show your expertise   
  

  * Several years of relevant experience in QA/QC roles within large-scale construction projects.
  * Proven experience in data centers, high-tech, or comparable complex construction environments.
  * Professional certification in Quality Management (e.g. Quality Engineer), additional qualifications such as Quality Auditor, Six Sigma, or Quality Process Analyst is preferred.
  * Practical Experience with databases for construction execution (e.g. BIM, EIDA, or comparable systems).
  * Very good English language skills, both written and spoken; German language skills are an advantage.
  * Willingness to travel within Germany as required by the project assignments.
  * Strong communication skills, enabling effective interaction with internal teams, subcontractors, and clients.
  * Ability to drive topics to completion with a clear focus, ensuring that issues are fully clarified and resolved.

What we offer you   
  

  * Are you looking for challenging projects? We offer you the opportunity to actively contribute to customized, large-scale engineering and construction projects.
  * Do you enjoy working in a multidisciplinary environment? Then Exyte is the right place for you.
  * Your start at Exyte: Benefit from a comprehensive onboarding program, on-the-job training, and a wide range of online and classroom-based training courses (e.g., German and English language training) available through our extensive Learning Management System.
  * Buddy System: Several weeks of dedicated support and close collaboration with your colleagues to help you settle into your new role.
  * Keep fit: Exyte subsidizes your fitness membership through EGYM Wellpass, giving you access to over 5,000 locations across Germany.
  * Do you want to actively shape the future? At Exyte, you will find the innovative and continuously growing environment you need to make an impact. So, what are you waiting for? Become part of this exciting journey!
