      Gehaltsbereich (von Envision Entertainment GmbH angegeben)

      Die Informationen zum Gehalt wurden von Envision Entertainment GmbH bereitgestellt. Das tatsächliche Gehalt ist abhängig von der Qualifikation und Berufserfahrung. Weitere Informationen erhalten Sie bei Ihrer Recruiterin oder Ihrem Recruiter.

      Bereich für das Grundgehalt

      500,00 €–500,00 €/Monat
      Your mission

      Your mission is to support our game development teams in creating new game experiences. You will work closely alongside the game design team & the programming team.

      The internship will consist mostly of the following:

      • Testing our game on functionality, quality and accuracy
      • Creating bug reports with our issue tracking tool JIRA
      • Communicating and synchronizing with our development team
      • Verifying bug fixes and testing new features versus game design description
      • Finding qualitative improvements of our game
      • Collecting, responding to and evaluating community feedback across our channels

      Your profile

      Must-haves:

      • Registered student or degree in computer science, media computer science or similar disciplines
      • German resident, able to work in our office in Ingelheim am Rhein, Rhineland-Palatinate
      • Logical understanding and ability to explain precisely in written descriptions
      • Ability to fluently communicate and document in the English language

      Preferable skills that you might have and that will be beneficial during your internship:

      • Experience with tools like JIRA and Perforce
      • Game design and/or interface affinity
      • Interest in build up strategy games

      Why us?

      • Cutting-edge technology and tools in a friendly working environment
      • Participation in one of Germany’s most experienced game development teams
      • A fun workplace and great co-workers
      • A nice office in Ingelheim, well connected to public transport
      • 500€/month, full-time (40h/week)

      This position is open from 12 October 2026 at the earliest. GERMAN RESIDENTS ONLY.

      About Us

      Envision Entertainment is a development studio in Ingelheim, Germany, led by veteran leadership with proven expertise in strategy and city builder off- and online games. Envision Entertainment may be seen as the rebirth of Phenomic Game Development (later: EA Phenomic). Our management team has been working together successfully in the games industry for more than 20 years, with Electronic Arts and as an independent studio. Our history includes games like The Settlers I + III, BattleForge and the SpellForce series as well as browser and mobile games like Command & Conquer: Tiberium Alliances and Path of War. Currently, Envision is working on Early Access PC build-up strategy game Pioneers of Pagonia.
