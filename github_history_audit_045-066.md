# Historical URL audit — positions 45–66

**Audit date:** 2026-09-24  
**Input:** `github_history_urls.json` → `unique_urls[44:66]` (1-based positions 45–66)  
**Rules applied:** `SPEC.md` dated 2026-09-24: QA/test role; direct vacancy card; posted within 30 days; Leipzig/Berlin/Dresden or another German city only with explicit remote/Home Office wording; German legal entity required.

## Method and result summary

Each URL was checked by at least two access methods:

1. `curl -L` with redirects followed (HTTP/final URL captured).
2. Independent page extraction or browser render.

All 22 URLs returned HTTP 200 without a final redirect. The second access exposed several semantic failures hidden by the 200 status: wrong vacancy at the same job ID, location-mode failures, stale postings, duplicate slugs, and insufficient employer-entity evidence.

`VERIFIED` means all hard gates were evidenced. `PARTIAL` means the vacancy itself is relevant/current but the German legal-entity gate was not sufficiently re-established during this audit. `REJECTED` means at least one hard gate failed.
